How to use: The scanner screens Kalshi sports markets for logical implication pairs (e.g. "Alcaraz wins French Open" → "Alcaraz wins a Grand Slam") — deterministic lattice rules auto-confirm the obvious ones, an LLM screens the rest into the Queue for your review. Confirmed pairs are re-evaluated against live orderbooks several times a day. When an evaluation finds a profitable trade, you get an email automatically — no need to poll Recs; that tab is history on demand.
